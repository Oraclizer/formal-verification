{\LARGE\bfseries\color{ink} Evidence Atomic Binding}\par
{\large Repository technical guide}\par
\vspace{3mm}

\textbf{Scope.} The \code{Evidence_Atomic_Binding} session specifies
evidence-qualified terminal decisions, their financial and regulatory
consumers, and the observations and recovery of those executions. It extends
\code{Preemptive_Lock_Correctness} without changing the parent's operation
semantics. An actual consumer here is the defined HOL transition or reader.
No general code-generation, compiled-runtime or deployment correspondence is
implied. The exact premises remain in the theory statements; see also
\source{README.md} and \source{model-contract.md}.

\section*{Identity and terminal authority}
The immutable source key $K$ belongs to a complete binding $B$: key, asset,
amount, destination, recipient, source epoch, operation and domain separator.
A route, worker generation, retry or new invocation identifier does not create
a new financial root. A source statement and a protocol decision are separate
values. \code{Observed} supplies no terminal decision; authenticated
\code{Finalized} and \code{Reversed} statements support the corresponding
confirmed and reversed record kinds.

\code{record_terminal} checks the certificate and operation-specific origin.
Monetary origin requires the exact positive source debit. A regulatory origin
uses the authenticated finalized regulatory statement. The first accepted
binding and kind remain fixed; reauthentication can add evidence for that same
core. One source profile cannot reinterpret the same key from finalized to
reversed. A lawful later action requires its own identity and effect duties.

These are source-attestation and record contracts. Distributed commit needs
agreement among participants, as treated by Gray and Lamport
\cite{grayLamport2006}; a local record update or source-fact threshold does not
provide that distributed implementation. Quorum, round, epoch changes and
restart remain obligations of the production terminal authority.

\section*{Source facts reach actual consumers}
The controlled source retains effect identity, available allocation and a
persistent no-effect fence. Applying the same exact binding is idempotent;
a successful fence prevents a delayed apply. Command arrival and acknowledgment
arrival are separate, so an effect can exist despite an unavailable reply.
Finalization and reversal are immutable alternatives after an effect. Reversal
restores the allocation once. An unknown outcome does not authorize a refund.

\code{issue_source_receipt} checks the current exact source fact and
signature-slot consistency. Receipt verification reads the issuance log.
The source-aware dispatcher consumes current receipts and effect/fence evidence;
arbitrary inputs are admitted to the dispatcher and rejected when guards fail.

\section*{One allocation, two accounting views}
For a fixed source pool, let $E$ be the finite set of controlled source effects,
$R$ the returned bindings and $C$ the bindings in the child credit history.
Define $U=E\setminus(R\cup C)$. The function $\operatorname{mass}$ sums binding
amounts belonging to this pool. Let $S_{\rm phys}$ be the authoritative source's
available units, $F$ the destination funding attributed to the pool's roots,
and $G$ its genesis allocation. Under the generated joint accounting contract:
\begin{align*}
 R\cap C&=\varnothing, & E&=U\mathbin{\dot\cup}C\mathbin{\dot\cup}R,\\
 S_{\rm phys}+\operatorname{mass}(U)+F&=G.
\end{align*}
Every credited binding retains its current finalized source fact. Returned
bindings have the incompatible reversed fact, so no binding can be in both
$C$ and $R$. The controlled source equation and this partition account for
the allocation without treating a returned amount as fresh funding.

The parent mirror separately satisfies
\[
 S_{\rm local}+U_{\rm local}+F=G,
\]
where $U_{\rm local}$ is its own unresolved mass. Debit and return gaps record
delayed mirroring. The physical source and its local mirror represent the same
allocation: adding their balances would double count. Independently consistent
fragments need not belong to one joint execution. A reversed source fragment
mixed with an unrelated credited fragment can count fifteen units against an
allocation of ten.

\section*{Root funding and actual decisions}
Destination balance is a finite sum of funding over financial roots. Thus,
for each key $k$ and destination account $a$,
\[
  \operatorname{funded}(k,a)\leq\operatorname{destination}(a).
\]
The parent contract also yields
\begin{align*}
 0\leq F&\leq G-S_{\rm local}\leq G,\\
 F=G-S_{\rm local}&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad U_{\rm local}=0,\\
 F=G&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
       S_{\rm local}=0\ \land\ U_{\rm local}=0.
\end{align*}
Zero unresolved mass need not mean an empty unresolved set. Funding is not
permission: an accepted descendant amount must be root-funded, but a current
policy can reject every positive amount despite available funding.

For an already credited root $b$, any finite parent action sequence that
excludes descendant actions consuming $b$ preserves its account funding and
filtered descendant history. Other roots can use the same asset and account.
The actual descendant reply is also preserved for the same context, request,
sender, recipient and amount. This does not preserve an old permission after
a policy change or after spending $b$.

For two states with financial history agreement and the same credited root,
equality of every holder's root funding is equivalent to equality of actual
descendant replies over \emph{all common} contexts, requests and quantities.
The reverse direction constructs a currently authorized separating probe.
A single restrictive policy is insufficient to recover funding.

Erasing only funding and descendant history from the reservation state loses
this distinction: two roots and two holder accounts can have equal erased
states but different actual continuation replies. Restricted one-root and
one-account results delimit this ambiguity. The erasure excludes the machine
journal; complete-journal reconstruction remains valid. It establishes no
globally minimal trace or sufficient representation for every operation.

\section*{Invocation, execution and completed replies}
The durable call model separates invocation, authority execution, response
collection and completion. An identifier has at most one durable execution
result, tied to an exact command, reply and source index. Reusing that identifier
with a conflicting command does not execute the new command. Crash and lost
response do not create a second execution of a recorded command.

\textbf{Old identifier and new attempt are different operations.}
Retransmission retrieves the existing result even if policy has since changed.
It does not reauthorize an old effect. A fresh invocation uses the actual current
dispatcher. Completed \code{Busy} and \code{Unavailable} remain completed with
those values; a later attempt for a different result requires a new identifier.

Real-time precedence distinguishes overlapping calls from a fresh call begun
after another has completed \cite{herlihyWing1990}. Here the specific ordering
result says: after a successful current reply completes, a fresh later invocation
cannot complete a successful current reply with an older revision. Overlapping
calls may deliver replies later. Balances need not be monotone. This ordering
result alone is not a linearizability theorem for every API and physical source.

\section*{Observation families retain different meanings}
The concrete readers inspect caches, histories and stored state. Independent
abstract readers retain application data, terminal receipts, publication,
phase, ownership, journal and revision as well as the financial/regulatory
product. Observation agreement is a theorem about these definitions, not a
definition that silently replaces every concrete reader with an abstract one.

{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.24\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
Reply family & Contract at the actual callback \\
\midrule
Current value & Cache equals the current complete logical snapshot and the
  query is ready. Relevant source effects and terminal records are published;
  application data also requires no active owner. \\
Protected value & Current reader permission, authority epoch, request scope
  and applicable terminal reference hold. Current application data also
  consumes the actual parent data-read transition. \\
Historical value & Requested revision identifies a ready snapshot captured
  by initialization or an actual callback. Protected history requires current
  permission to access that historical data. \\
Raw value or journal & Explicit stored fields or journal events, without
  a coherent-current label or confidentiality guarantee for public storage. \\
Operational status & Reservation phase and reported secondary progress.
  Reporting progress is not settlement execution. \\
Physical inspection & Actual controlled source units or effect history,
  subject to source availability. \\
Failure reply & Actual busy, unavailable or rejected branch. Completion
  preserves that result exactly as it preserves a successful result. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}}

Unprotected current and historical queries cover source and destination
balances. Root funding, application data, regulatory state and terminal
receipts use the protected path. The raw path remains exposed.

\code{current_cache_valid} compares the complete snapshot, including
function-valued state. Equal revision numbers alone do not authenticate values.
This is a logical current-state contract, not an established code-generation
or constant-cost network mechanism. Refresh can be invalidated by an intervening
authority revision.

Current source-balance queries additionally require source availability and
zero debit and return reconciliation gaps. A lost source acknowledgment can
therefore yield \code{Busy} despite a refreshed local cache. Secondary lag is
separate telemetry: a ready endpoint can consume primary state before secondary
settlement, while a stale endpoint cannot present its cache as current.

\section*{Core product words and independent response relations}
The transfer ledger has independently defined balances, root funding and
debit, credit, return and descendant histories. Its event arithmetic is compared
with actual reservation fields, committed events and journal suffixes.
Regulatory synchronization supplies a separate action word. Token quantity
and regulatory holding support are not identified.

Actual observation and source-aware callbacks determine a finite word of core
operations. A query or rejected source admission may stutter. Accepted core
operations consume the existing financial and regulatory functions. Generated
parent contracts and a valid regulatory initial state supply the applicable
premises. An arbitrary arithmetic ledger event is not thereby a legal protocol
execution.

The richer \code{source_observation_alpha} retains physical source units,
effects and returns. A source effect with a lost acknowledgment can change
these fields while leaving the whole local observation state unchanged.
\textbf{The product-word result describes the core projection, not a full
transition simulation of this richer target.} The target is not a classifier
for every API reply either: availability, caches, historical cuts, secondary
progress and permission inputs remain explicit in response relations.

Every completed sourced reply is tied to the actual indexed callback from
the authoritative replay prefix. Current values and revisions agree with that
execution cut. For a completed historical response, actual capture provenance
supplies indices satisfying
\[
  \text{snapshot index}\leq\text{execution index}<\text{journal length}.
\]
The independent historical value and revision come from that earlier cut.
Specific relations retain raw and operational fields, physical availability
and completed failures. Sourced callbacks are analyzed directly, rather than
inheriting a theorem about another callback/state/reply type.

\section*{Recovery preserves effects and current consumers}
\code{checked_current_replay} compares the candidate's genesis and exact
journal with the current authority, then validates recorded callback history.
Internal replayability alone does not establish authenticity, completeness or
freshness. Applicable checks reject shortened, reordered, conflicting,
duplicated and wrong-genesis candidates.

Accepted replay restores the actual authority state, including terminal
records and descendant lineage. Sourced recovery also preserves the physical
source projection and resumes through the source-aware dispatcher. Restoration
does not create a credit or refund. Old identifiers retrieve their durable
results; new consumption uses current guards. The authentic complete current
journal and its persistence remain physical implementation obligations.

\section*{Conditional progress and concrete controls}
A fresh, uninterrupted call at a connected, available endpoint has a finite
begin/dispatch/collect/complete program. Its remaining length decreases, and
it completes the actual callback reply, including a failure. A ready public
read succeeds after refresh in a quiescent interval. Retry fairness and network
connectivity alone are insufficient: intervening context updates can invalidate
every refresh through arbitrarily long retry sequences. The completion theorem
does not guarantee terminal success. Separate conditional terminal programs
start in a valid joint state and consume a current controlled terminal fact,
an admissible exact certificate, an available issuance slot, a positive
mirrored debit and an empty record slot. Current credit admission
and an unused marker, or current reservation ownership, pending phase and
relay epoch, supply the remaining conditions. Five delivered source-aware
actions issue evidence, record the decision, publish the certificate, execute
credit or return, and publish primary state, without intervening source or
context changes. A separate fresh durable call completes the final core
publication response; it does not turn every action into a completed
source-aware client call. Eventual evidence and delivery remain external.
Unknown source effects are never made refundable to force progress.

Normal paths include bypass credit, reversed outcome, exact recovery and a
monetary primary followed by actual regulatory freeze, ordinary-transfer
rejection, authorized enforcement and reconciliation. The regulatory scenario
uses a separate supplied abstract source fact. Removing particular terminal,
source-finalization, cache, source-gap or current-journal checks exposes bad
effects or observations. Raw intermediate values can be neither atomic endpoint
state. These are formal-function controls, not compiled-product mutation tests.

\section*{Implementation duties and nonclaims}
The logical authority owns current endpoint context and regulatory state.
Submitted caller, authority epoch and version remain subject to the parent's
current-use checks. An implementation must bind those checks to the actual
effect. A candidate proof, prepare, durable decision, local effect, primary
publication and secondary settlement are distinct events.

Primary publication requires the relevant recorded effect. It does not assert
that every physical domain has already applied it. A product contract requiring
all-domain application before that same publication must reconcile its timing
with consumption before secondary settlement; the model does not silently
equate those conditions.

{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.23\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
Boundary & Evidence required from the implementation \\
\midrule
Terminal authority & Production primary-finality record (PFR) producer and
  verifier binding exact fields to signer identity, uniqueness and threshold,
  with persistent agreement across round, quorum, epoch change and restart. \\
Source adapter & Immutable effect identity, atomic deduplication, persistent
  fences, authenticated current outcomes, lost-acknowledgment recovery and
  one-time returns in the actual bank, chain or DAML API. \\
Durable calls and recovery & Atomic effect/result recording, exact invocation
  identity, retained completed failures and an authentic, complete, current
  genesis/journal or justified checkpoint source. \\
Current consumption & Actual authority and dependency ownership plus a
  barrier, fence or equivalent mechanism coupling the current check to use. \\
Public observations & Correspondence for declared APIs, raw getters, storage
  and events, including historical revision/timestamp mapping and actual
  primary application and secondary settlement. \\
Compiled consumer & Exact source, ABI, encoding, hash preimage, compiler
  target and runtime consumer, supported by normal execution and a specific
  guard-removal control for each required connection. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}}

The inspected token already checks action identity/lifecycle, validity
interval, current authority, dependencies and nonce; reversal also checks the
current original effect. Those defenses must be preserved and connected
field by field. Token replay rejection is not automatically retransmission of
a completed formal reply. A distinct reversal receipt and changed action or
case lifecycle are not a same-key change of the model's stable source fact.
The settlement seam accepts external primary-finality and finalized-receipt
providers. Its authorization result does not itself execute a CDK application
or declare secondary settlement complete.

\source{product-obligations.json} records source findings, planned tests and
unverified compiled consumers. \source{product-api-inventory.json} is a source
declaration inventory, not a compiler-derived ABI certificate. No public API,
raw storage surface or event is removed to make the formal statements hold.

\textbf{Nonclaims.} This contract supplies no deployment conformance,
universal source support, unconditional termination, universal raw-observation
atomicity, complete runtime refinement, global information characterization
or realization of arbitrary records. It also makes no general claim that
function-valued snapshot comparisons are code-generatable. The source guide
in \source{README.md} locates the theory interfaces; the exact conditions are
stated in \source{model-contract.md} and the referenced theories.
