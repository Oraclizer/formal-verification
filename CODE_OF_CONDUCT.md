# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone
participating in technical, academic, and community discussion, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a constructive community include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- challenging claims, proofs, and assumptions with technical specificity;
- distinguishing people from the work under review;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- respecting disclosure, confidentiality, authorship, and citation norms;
- focusing on what is best for rigorous and reproducible scholarship.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory comments;
- sexualized language or attention;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith misrepresentation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliating against a good-faith counterexample or vulnerability report;
- plagiarism, fabricated evidence, or deliberate removal of attribution;
- disclosing sensitive vulnerability details outside the private process.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `jay@oraclizer.io`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, close or lock interactions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate,
and separate from whether a participant's technical criticism is correct.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially
represents the project in public. It does not govern independent academic
criticism made without project representation.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
